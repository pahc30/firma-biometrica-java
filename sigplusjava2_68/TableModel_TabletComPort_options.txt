----setTabletComPort() options----
For Topaz USB-connected ("-HSB") pads, use:
"HID1"

For Topaz 9-pin serial pads (under Windows) use:
"COMX"
(where X is the port in question, for instance: COM1)

For Linux and 9-pin serial pads, for instance:
"/dev/ttyS0"
